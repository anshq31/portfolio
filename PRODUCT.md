## Brand Commitments

Scroll motion is part of the identity: pieces of the engineering map assemble as it enters view and separate as the visitor moves away; small material markers echo that motion in experience and project sections. Keep all copy readable throughout and respect prefers-reduced-motion with a stable layout.

The KYROS Software biometric platform is labeled “PalmVein Verification” in public-facing portfolio copy at the user's request. Do not use the prior internal product name in the portfolio.

## Product Principles

1. Specificity over adjectives — "29 REST endpoints over 11 JPA entities" beats "scalable backend." Every project card leads with a concrete, numeric fact from the resume.
2. Ownership is the pitch — surface where he owned end-to-end work (PalmVein Verification, Sports Connect backend) distinctly from where he integrated into an existing system (AppVersal keyboard).
3. No fabricated evidence — no invented testimonials, client logos, screenshots, or metrics beyond what the resume states.
4. One CTA path per audience, not a forced merge — resume download and email/LinkedIn serve both audiences without needing separate "hire me" vs "recruit me" tracks.
