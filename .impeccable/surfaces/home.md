## Direction contract

THESIS: An engineer's portfolio as a system ledger: a clear map of the Android, Spring Boot, native, and data layers that make the work function.

OWN-WORLD: Warm paper and deep ink carry the page, with muted pine for Android work and brass for system details. Every surface uses solid color. Manrope carries readable editorial copy; DM Mono is reserved for dates, technologies, and measured context. Fine rules and square material blocks echo engineering flow diagrams. No avatar or portrait, neon, gradients, or decorative blur.

STORY: Recruiters and collaborators see Ansh's Android and Java work through concrete ownership, production evidence, and two direct résumé downloads. The flow map makes the work's architecture visible while project details remain direct and factual. The biometric project is called PalmVein Verification.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A sticky navigation anchors the page. Ansh's name, Android + Java role, short evidence-led summary, and résumé links lead on the left. A labeled engineering flow map sits on the right, connecting Android, Spring Boot, native integration, and PostgreSQL. On narrow viewports the map stacks below the introduction.

FORM: A responsive editorial layout with timeline work entries, a four-project field, grouped skill rows, education and community evidence, and direct contact links. Surfaces are flat and square; fine rules and compact technology tags establish hierarchy.

MOTION: IntersectionObserver assembles and separates the map nodes as they enter or leave the viewport; small material markers echo the behavior in work and project sections. Text remains readable in every state. Reduced-motion preferences present the stable layout without moving parts.

SEED: d598cff1. The user-pinned constraints select the shipped direction; no quality-bar image was used as a mockup.
